## Featured AI Projects

### [Provenance](https://provenance.arielmagalso.com/demo) — Verified RAG System

AI-generated answers can be inaccurate or difficult to verify. Provenance retrieves information from approved documents and produces grounded answers with sentence-level citations, visible evidence, and safe refusal when the sources do not support an answer.

**Stack:** Next.js, TypeScript, RAG, Claude, Supabase

[Live Demo](https://provenance.arielmagalso.com/demo) · [Source Code](https://github.com/ArielMagalsoDev/provenance)

---

### [LedgerGuard](https://ledgerguard.arielmagalso.com/demo) — AI Invoice Review Automation

Manual invoice reviews are time-consuming and can miss duplicate charges, inconsistencies, and policy violations. LedgerGuard extracts invoice evidence, applies deterministic financial controls, flags exceptions, and requires human approval before action.

**Stack:** Python, FastAPI, Claude, PostgreSQL, Docker

[Live Demo](https://ledgerguard.arielmagalso.com/demo) · [Source Code](https://github.com/ArielMagalsoDev/ledgerguard)

---

### [Verdict](https://verdict.arielmagalso.com/demo) — AI Lead Qualification Automation

Sales teams often review inbound leads using incomplete or inconsistent information. Verdict verifies evidence before scoring, applies deterministic qualification rules, explains every decision, and escalates uncertain cases for human review.

**Stack:** Python, FastAPI, Claude, PostgreSQL, Docker

[Live Demo](https://verdict.arielmagalso.com/demo) · [Source Code](https://github.com/ArielMagalsoDev/verdict)
